# Crimson Moonflare — Songs to Generate

Band identity: metalcore / alternative rock. Raw, independent, no label.
Themes across all songs: mental health, identity, rebellion, emotional weight.
Vocal style: aggressive screams with melodic clean choruses (mixed vocals).
Instrumentation: heavy downtuned guitars, driving bass, fast/precise drums, no synths.

---

## EP: Vein & Voltage (May 2025)

---

### 01 — Ignite the Wound
**Duration target:** 3:42
**Role:** EP opener, first single (Nov 2024)

**Background:**
Written in a van on the way back from the band's first rehearsal. Never revised. Riff-driven, opens hard. Slow devastating outro. Recorded in one afternoon, second take kept.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, fast tempo, heavy downtuned guitars, aggressive screamed verses, melodic clean chorus, precise double bass drums, raw production, emotional, powerful outro that slows to a crawl, no synths, live band feel

Song structure: fast aggressive intro riff → screamed verse → melodic chorus with clean vocals → second verse → big chorus → bridge slows down into a heavy half-time breakdown → slow devastating outro with clean vocals fading out
```

---

### 02 — Scarlet Static
**Duration target:** 4:11
**Role:** Second single (Mar 2025), EP track 2

**Background:**
Louder and stranger than track 1. Has a 30-second mid-section the band almost cut three times — dissonant, long, indulgent — that may be the best part of the whole record. Lo-fi live energy.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, loud, heavier than previous track, downtuned guitars, screamed verses, melodic chorus, raw lo-fi texture, dissonant and experimental mid-section that breaks the rhythm entirely, builds back into a crushing final chorus, no polish, sounds like a live room recording

Song structure: heavy riff intro → aggressive verse → melodic chorus → second verse → chorus → unusual dissonant ambient breakdown (30 seconds, unsettling, almost removes all rhythm) → explosion back into the heaviest version of the chorus → short bleak outro
```

---

### 03 — Below the Signal
**Duration target:** 3:58
**Role:** EP track 3, mid-album breather before the final stretch

**Background:**
Sits between the intensity of the opening two tracks and the more introspective closing pair. Gives the listener room before pulling them back in. Thematic focus on disconnection and being unable to reach someone.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, mid-tempo, dynamic contrast between quiet verses and explosive choruses, clean sung verses with understated guitar, full-band heavy chorus with screamed backing vocals over clean lead, themes of emotional distance and disconnection, raw production, slightly more melodic than the first two tracks but still heavy

Song structure: quiet clean guitar intro → soft clean verse → chorus explodes with full-band crash → second verse stays quiet → second chorus hits harder → melodic bridge with both clean and screamed vocal layers → final chorus with maximum intensity → quiet clean guitar outro mirroring the intro
```

---

### 04 — Glass Meridian
**Duration target:** 3:27
**Role:** EP track 4, the most melodic moment of the record

**Background:**
Shorter and more focused. Introspective — deals with identity and the moment you realise you've been lying to yourself. More melody-forward than any other track, but still rooted in heavy guitars. The band described writing this as unexpectedly honest.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, melodic, mid-tempo, clean vocals dominate with screaming used sparingly for impact, heavy guitars that support rather than overpower the melody, emotional and introspective, themes of self-deception and identity, anthemic chorus, raw but controlled production

Song structure: melodic clean intro riff → clean sung verse, reflective tone → anthemic chorus, full band, mostly clean vocals → second verse, more urgent → second chorus → short screamed breakdown, the only moment of pure aggression in the song → final chorus with both clean and screamed vocals layered → fade
```

---

### 05 — The Last Weight
**Duration target:** 3:09
**Role:** EP closer (track 5), shortest track

**Background:**
The EP's conclusion. Deals with letting go of something — a person, a feeling, a version of yourself. Starts stripped back and builds to the heaviest moment on the entire record before cutting out abruptly. No outro. Ends mid-breath.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, slow build, starts quiet and sparse with only clean vocals and single guitar, gradually layers in bass then drums then full heavy guitars, themes of release and finality, cathartic, the heaviest and most crushing moment comes at the very end before an abrupt hard cut-off — no fade, no outro, just stops

Song structure: sparse intro — single clean guitar, clean vocals only → bass enters slowly → drums enter at half the expected tempo → first heavy chorus hits with full band → quiet verse again, more fragile → second chorus heavier than first → the final section is the heaviest thing on the EP — full drop, screamed vocals, full band at maximum intensity for 30 seconds → hard cut to silence mid-phrase, no resolution
```

## Standalone Singles

### Hollow Signal
**Duration target:** 3:55
**Role:** Standalone single (Dec 2024), released between first single and EP

**Background:**
Released one month after Ignite the Wound and a week after the debut show in Zurich. Not on the EP. Feels like a companion piece to the first single — same energy but more atmospheric. Deals with numbness and the feeling of broadcasting into nothing — sending messages nobody receives.

**Suno prompt:**
```
metalcore, alternative rock, atmospheric, mid-to-fast tempo, heavy guitars with a slightly more spacious mix than typical metalcore, screamed verses with melodic clean chorus, themes of numbness and isolation, lyrics about reaching out and receiving nothing back, raw live production, slightly more reverb on guitars than the EP tracks giving a hollow resonant quality, no synths

Song structure: single reverb-heavy guitar intro, almost empty-sounding → fast aggressive verse with screamed vocals → melodic chorus, clean vocals, unexpectedly catchy → second verse, heavier guitar layer added → second chorus → atmospheric breakdown — guitars drop to a single repeating figure, vocals half-spoken half-sung about disconnection → final chorus with added second vocal harmony layer → abrupt end
```

## Notes for Generation

- **Consistency tip:** Generate all tracks in the same Suno session or save the style tags from the first successful result and reuse them across all songs so the sonic palette stays coherent.
- **Vocals:** If Suno lets you specify, ask for a male-leaning voice that can switch between harsh screams and clear melodic singing in the same track.
- **Production feel:** Aim for something that sounds like it was recorded in a real room — not over-produced. "Basement recorded, mixed like it belongs in an arena" is the internal brief.
- **Tempo reference:** Tracks 1, 2, 6 are the fastest. Track 3 is mid-tempo. Track 4 is melodic/mid. Track 5 starts slow and ends heavy.
